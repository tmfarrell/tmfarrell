# Hi, I'm Tim Farrell 👋

**Senior Product Manager at [OM1](https://www.om1.com/)**, building data and AI/ML products for biology and medicine.

I lead products built on large-scale real-world data and clinical AI (e.g., billions of clinical records, 100s of models), from concept to commercial impact. I came to product from engineering: about 4 years building genomics and data systems at the Broad Institute and Day Zero Diagnostics.

### 🔭 What I work on
- **Clinical AI:** taking a proprietary foundation model from internal capability to an externally consumable product, e.g. the [PhenOM API](https://phenom-docs.om1.com/)
- **Real-world data platforms:** EHR/EMR and claims data, FHIR / SMART on FHIR, ML/AI enrichment (extraction + imputation)
- **0-to-1 product and GTM** across payer, provider and life sciences markets
